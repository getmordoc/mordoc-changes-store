# Close the mobile menu when the window becomes wide

## Description
As a reader, I want the page to stay usable when I widen the window while the mobile menu is open, so that I can keep reading and scrolling.

## Why
- When the mobile menu is open and the window becomes wide, for example by widening the browser or turning a tablet, the whole page turns dark and no longer scrolls, until the reader reloads it.
- The menu stays open in the background, although a wide window shows the sidebar instead of the mobile menu.

## What
- The mobile menu closes as soon as the window becomes wide enough for the sidebar, and the page looks and scrolls as usual.
